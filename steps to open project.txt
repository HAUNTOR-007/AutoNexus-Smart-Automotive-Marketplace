========================================================================
🚀 AUTO NEXUS - HOW TO RUN & DEMONSTRATE ON XAMPP LOCALHOST
========================================================================

1. START XAMPP SERVICES:
   - Open XAMPP Control Panel.
   - Start both "Apache" and "MySQL" modules (both green).

2. DATABASE IMPORT (phpMyAdmin):
   - Open your browser and navigate to:
     👉 http://localhost/phpmyadmin/
   - The database `autonexus` is already created and ready.
   - If importing freshly on any computer:
     Click "Import" -> Choose `autonexus.sql` located in this folder -> Click "Import".
   - Direct database console link:
     👉 http://localhost/phpmyadmin/index.php?route=/database/structure&db=autonexus

3. LAUNCH AUTO NEXUS MODULES:
   🏠 Homepage & Showroom:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/index.html

   🛠️ Admin Control Panel:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/admin.html
   (Manage live showroom vehicles, accessories catalogue, customer inquiries, and users)

   🏎️ New Cars Inventory:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/new-cars.html
   (Explore showroom models and book instant test-drives synced to MySQL)

   🚗 Sell Your Car (Valuation Request):
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/sell-your-car.html
   (Submit vehicle valuation and sale requests saved directly to MySQL)

   🌍 Import On Demand:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/import-on-demand.html
   (Submit international vehicle sourcing and import requests)

   💎 Performance & Styling Accessories:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/accessories.html
   (Browse upgrade catalogue, direct ordering, and custom inquiries)

   📊 Car Valuation Calculator:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/car-valuation.html
   (Interactive algorithmic market valuation tool with direct expert consultation booking)

   🧠 Smart Buy Advisory:
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/smart-buy-advisory.html
   (Intelligent multi-factor vehicle recommendation and comparison engine)

   🔐 Authentication (Login & Sign In):
   👉 http://localhost/autonexus/login.html
   - Admin Credentials:
     Email: admin@autonexus.com
     Password: admin123
   - Customer Accounts:
     Switch to "Sign In" tab to register any new account (persisted in MySQL `users` table).
